About

The art of finding signal in noise is what first drew me to machine learning and competitive data science. A result is rarely interesting to me on its own. I want to know what led to it, where it falls short, and what might make it better.

I am pursuing BS in Data Science and Applications at IIT Madras and work as a Research Consultant at WorldQuant. My interests have since grown beyond modelling into quantitative research and software development. I like exploring ideas, questioning assumptions, and occasionally disappearing down a rabbit hole just to understand how something works.

I also enjoy the process of turning ideas into things people can actually use. Not everything needs to be a research problem; sometimes building something is the best way to understand it.

Away from all that, I am usually watching football (Liverpool, of course), reading a book, or getting absorbed in a good TV series.
